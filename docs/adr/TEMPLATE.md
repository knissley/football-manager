# NNNN. Title in the imperative

**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [ADR-NNNN](NNNN-....md)
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What's the situation that forces a decision? Facts and constraints, not opinions.
What breaks if we don't decide?

## Decision

What we're doing, stated plainly and in the active voice. "We will …"

## Consequences

What becomes easier. What becomes harder. What we're now committed to. Be honest
about the costs — an ADR that lists only benefits isn't recording a decision, it's
recording a preference.

## Alternatives considered

Each real alternative and the specific reason it lost. If there was no alternative,
this probably didn't need an ADR.
