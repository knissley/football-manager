# A comment for #227, not an issue: part of the slope may be selection

Post as a comment on #227. It changes nothing about #227's finding; it names a confound
its re-measurement should account for.

---

From `docs/audit-pre-snap-exchange.md` (C34, #229), §5 and §8.

#227 reads the engine's yards-per-carry response to the box as a slope nine to fourteen
times too steep: 3.1 / 3.2 outnumbered by one against 4.7 / 4.8 even, where the sport's
gap is 4.3–4.6 against 4.5–4.7.

**Part of the sport's small gap is selection, not physics.** In the feed the carries that
meet a loaded box are the ones the offence chose to keep on against it, and the runs it
checked out of are dropbacks, not carries. The audit measured that the engine chooses
nothing: eleven personnel on first and ten runs at 56–60% into base and 58–62% into nickel
(400 games, both seeds), where the feed runs 58.7% into a four-back front and 41.9% into
five or more (ADR-0014's counts, by Bayes; 58.4 / 42.4 in 2024). So the engine's
outnumbered carries are an unselected sample of every run call, and the sport's are the
subset an offence kept on. Some of the slope is a constant that is wrong; some may be a
check that is missing.

**Ask.** When #227 re-measures, it should either wait for 05 (the check) to land and
re-take the three boxes on a selected sample, or state that its band is partly a
selection effect the engine cannot reach without one and grade the level with that named.
Which of those is not this comment's to decide; that the confound exists is.
