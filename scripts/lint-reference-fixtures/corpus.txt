THE LAWS OF THE GARDEN RELAY

*** THIS IS NOT A RULEBOOK. ***

Every sentence below was invented for this fixture. Nothing here is quoted from,
paraphrased from, or derived from any real rulebook, and the sport it describes does
not exist. It is here so scripts/lint-reference.sh --self-test has a corpus to find
reproductions in without the repository holding a line of anybody's copyrighted text.

It imitates the shape of a rulebook and nothing else: numbered rules, numbered
sections, numbered articles, and one heading deliberately broken across a line the way
a PDF text extractor breaks them, so the script's repair of that is exercised too.

RULE 7 THE BATON
SECTION 3 EXCHANGES
ARTICLE 1. LEGAL EXCHANGE. A runner may hand the baton to a team mate only while both
of them stand inside the painted exchange box, and the exchange is finished at the
moment the receiving runner holds the baton alone.

ARTICLE 2. END OF THE RELAY. The relay is over when the anchor runner crosses the
painted finish stripe while holding the baton in either hand. A crossing made without
the baton ends nothing and the anchor must return for it.

SEC
TION 4 PENALTIES
ARTICLE 1. DROPPED BATON. A dropped baton must be recovered by the runner who dropped
it and by nobody else on that team, and the recovery may be made from any lane.

Penalty: For an exchange outside the painted box: the team is moved back one place in
the finishing order, and where two teams are both moved back they keep their order
relative to each other.

The judges keep a written record of every exchange and of every dropped baton, and
that record is read aloud to the captains before the results are posted.
